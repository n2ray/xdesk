# XDesk 0.6.4

[中文说明见下方](#xdesk-064-1)

Notifications made dependable, one look for every permission prompt, smoother Macs (also through Tailscale), and a round of security, battery and bug fixes from three audits of XDesk's foundations. It installs over 0.6.3.

## Fixed
- **The notification centre was sometimes empty.** Granting notification access is not enough: the system must also start XDesk's notification service. On HyperOS, after XDesk's process restarted (an update, the system closing it, a reboot), the system's attempt was refused because XDesk may not start itself, and no notification came in until something rebound it. XDesk now asks the system again when the service stops, when XDesk comes to the front, when the external screen appears and when the panel opens. While it is not connected, the panel says so and opens the phone's Autostart settings for you (Settings › Notifications shows it too).
- On every connection the centre is made the same as the phone's notifications: ones cleared meanwhile leave, new ones come in quietly.
- Clearing a notification from the external screen while the service was not connected made it vanish there but stay on the phone; it now stays in both.
- Notifications whose importance the system did not report were treated as silent and not shown. They are shown now.
- Some apps post only a group summary; those notifications were never shown. A summary now shows when its group has no messages.
- Chat notifications often showed only the app's name: the text now comes from the conversation's newest message, the big text or the inbox lines when the plain text is empty.
- Incoming calls (ongoing) now show.
- An empty panel says how many notifications on the phone are not shown here (silent, ongoing, or from apps set not to show).
- Apps set to "not shown" can be found again in the per-app settings.
- **App name only** now applies to the panel's list too, not just the banners, so a projector does not show what the banners hide. ▾ still shows a notification in full when you ask.
- The Do not disturb tile is on in any of the phone's DND modes, and turning it off and on returns to the mode you had, instead of switching to total silence.
- Opening a notification waits for the app to appear on the external screen (up to 2 seconds) instead of a fixed 0.6 seconds, so it is not started twice; when it cannot open there, or an action is refused, a banner says so.
- The panel keeps open and selected rows when the list changes, and no longer redraws for every battery percent. If its window could not be added, it can be opened again.

## Changed
- **Every "turn this permission on" looks the same now**, on the phone and on the external screen: an amber card with a key, "Turn on «X»", one line on why, one line on where it is in the phone's settings, and an amber button ("Turn on ›", or "Open on the phone ›" on the external screen). Red stays for errors, grey for notes. When you come back with the permission on, the card turns green and goes. This covers notification access, Autostart, Do Not Disturb access, notifications, display over other apps, running Termux commands, Shizuku, the camera for pairing a Mac and the setting USB tethering needs.
- Before the system asks for notifications, for running Termux commands or for the camera, XDesk now says what for first. The desktop window no longer puts a second, unexplained notification request on top, and phones before Android 13 are not asked at all.
- Allowed on the app's details page after refusing for good, starting a desktop, listing Termux or stopping goes on by itself when you come back. "Not now" on Do Not Disturb access is for this time only; the next tap asks again, and the external screen says to look at the phone.

## Macs: smoother, also through Tailscale
- **At home, the home door.** XDesk finds a paired Mac on the local network (Bonjour) and connects through Sill's home door, which streams at the home quality and recovers from a dropped frame at once; elsewhere it uses the saved address as before. A Mac paired before this version has no recognition key yet: pair it again once to use it.
- **Away (Tailscale, the internet), at most 60 fps**, and always at the external screen's current refresh rate rather than its highest. The same quality needs half the data.
- Decoded pictures go to the screen the moment they are ready instead of with the next message; the decoder is set to the stream's real size and full speed.
- A dead connection is noticed in about 6 s instead of 12, a new network (Wi-Fi to mobile, VPN) reconnects at once, and while a Mac is on screen the pause between attempts is at most 2 s.
- Reconnecting to what the Mac already streams no longer restarts its capture (up to 2 s of frozen picture); a quality mode is one change, not three.
- Video traffic class, display and audio thread priorities, Wi-Fi low-latency mode while a Mac is shown; low-latency sound whose delay no longer grows after a hiccup.

## Security
- **Wireless display**: XDesk now accepts a receiver only after it proves it holds the pairing (or you just paired with its code). Before, a device on the same network announcing a known receiver's id could get the picture and sound, send input and replace the pairing.
- **The X server no longer listens on TCP or on the abstract socket** that every app on the phone can reach (`-nolisten tcp -nolisten local`). Programs in Termux, proot and chroot connect as before through Termux's own socket.
- **No clipboard text or typed characters in the system log** any more (the native code wrote them at debug level), nor whole key events.
- **Another app can no longer freeze XDesk** by handing it a Binder that never answers: the X connection calls run off the main thread with a timeout, pending ones are bounded, and on Android 14+ only Termux, XDesk, root and shell are taken. The X server's Binder answers only XDesk.
- **DRI3 buffers from X programs are checked** for size and overflow before they are mapped; a malformed one is refused instead of crashing the X server or writing past its buffer.
- **The web display and XDesk Receiver are reachable only on Wi-Fi, Ethernet and USB tethering**: no longer from this phone itself, from VPNs (Tailscale included) or mobile data, as the docs always said. A device that has not paired is no longer told the receiver's name.
- "Send to computer" asks before sending, whichever app opened it. The old Termux:X11 preferences screen is no longer open to other apps.

## Battery and speed
- **The X server sleeps when nothing is connected**: it was woken at the phone's refresh rate (120 times a second) also with no window, and announced itself to XDesk every second for its whole life.
- **No wake lock while nothing streams**: the dashboard or a listening web display kept the CPU awake all night. Now only a running desktop, app session or stream holds it.
- Desktop sound pauses after half a second of silence instead of playing silence for the whole session.
- Pasting or typing long text no longer freezes the phone (2.5 ms per character on the main thread); a key press no longer waits 60 ms each time; mouse moves and scrolls no longer pile up behind a slow injection; external mice and keyboards are read without a 200 ms poll; `dumpsys` runs at most every 2.5 s instead of every second.
- The desktop window no longer runs the accelerometer, and its watchdog runs every second instead of five times a second. Touching a still picture no longer redraws the whole screen 120 times a second. The X server is pinned to the CPUs it was meant for. The notification panel builds an expanded notification only when you open it.

## Other fixes
- Long clipboard text from Linux arrived cut and could desynchronise the connection; events from several threads could interleave. The connection is now read and written whole.
- Closing and reopening the desktop window could leave it black (the old connection's end dropped the new one); a desktop window closed while connecting kept retrying and leaking.
- The 5% low-battery banner never came after the 15% one.
- After a banner with an open reply went away, every key, Esc included, was swallowed.
- Coming back to XDesk after a minute could leave the phone screen dimmed to 1%.
- Changing several settings at once ran their handling once per setting; a few leaks of windows and threads; a buffer whose lock failed stayed locked for good.

## Upgrading
- Install the APK over 0.6.3, and run `xdesk update` in Termux (the X server's start changed). Update XDesk Receiver too. The Mac app is unchanged apart from the version number.
- If the panel says notifications are not coming in, turn on Autostart for XDesk on the phone once.
- To use a Mac's home door, pair it again once (Settings › Macs). Until then it is reached at its saved address as before.
- If you used the web display or XDesk Receiver over a VPN or mobile data, that no longer works by design; use Wi-Fi, Ethernet or USB tethering.

# XDesk 0.6.4

外屏通知改得可靠了，所有权限提示统一成一个样子，Mac 更流畅（走 Tailscale 也是），另外对 XDesk 底层做了三份审计，修了一批安全、耗电和 bug 问题。覆盖安装在 0.6.3 上即可。

## 修复
- **通知中心有时是空的。** 只给通知使用权还不够，系统还要启动 XDesk 的通知服务。HyperOS 上，XDesk 的进程一重启（装新版、被系统关掉、重启手机），系统去启动这个服务时会因为 XDesk 没有「自启动」权限而被拒绝，之后一条通知都进不来，直到碰巧重新连上。现在服务断开时、XDesk 回到前台时、外屏出现时、打开面板时，XDesk 都会再向系统请求。没连上时面板会明说，并能直接在手机上打开自启动设置（设置 › 通知里也有一行状态）。
- 每次连上时，通知中心都和手机上的通知对一遍：期间清掉的拿掉，新来的悄悄加进来。
- 服务没连上时在外屏上清除通知，外屏上没了、手机上却还在；现在两边都保留。
- 系统没报重要性的通知被当成静默通知不显示，现在会显示。
- 有的应用只发分组摘要，这类通知以前从不显示；现在组里没有消息时摘要会显示。
- 聊天通知常常只剩应用名：正文为空时，现在从会话的最新一条、长文本或收件箱样式的行里取。
- 来电（常驻通知）现在会显示。
- 面板为空时，会说明手机上还有几条没显示在这里（静默、常驻，或来自设为不显示的应用）。
- 设成「不显示」的应用，在按应用的设置里还能找到、改回来。
- **只显示应用名**现在也管面板的列表，不只是横幅，投影机上不会显示横幅藏起来的内容。点 ▾ 仍可看全文。
- 「勿扰」方块在手机的任何勿扰模式下都算开；关掉再打开会回到原来的模式，不再变成完全静音。
- 打开一条通知时，等应用真的出现在外屏上（最多 2 秒），不再固定等 0.6 秒，所以不会启动两次；开不到外屏或操作被应用拒绝时，会弹横幅说明。
- 列表变化时，展开和选中的条目保持不变；电量每变 1% 不再重画整个列表。面板窗口加不上时，下次还能再打开。

## 改动
- **所有「去开权限」的提示统一成一个样子**，手机屏和外屏都一样：琥珀色卡片加钥匙图标，写「需要打开「X」」，一行说为什么，一行说在手机设置的哪里，再加一个琥珀色按钮（「去开启 ›」，外屏上是「在手机上打开 ›」）。红色只用于出错，灰色只用于说明。开好权限回来，卡片变绿后消失。涵盖通知使用权、自启动、勿扰权限、通知、悬浮窗、运行 Termux 命令、Shizuku、配对 Mac 用的相机，以及 USB 网络共享要的系统设置权限。
- 系统弹窗问通知、运行 Termux 命令、相机权限之前，XDesk 先说明要它做什么。桌面窗口不再另外弹一个没有说明的通知权限框，Android 13 以前的手机不再问。
- 选过"不再询问"后在应用详情页里允许了，回来时原本要做的事（启动桌面、列出 Termux 环境、停止）会接着做。勿扰权限选"以后再说"只管这一次，下次点还会问，外屏上会提示去手机上看。

## Mac：更流畅，走 Tailscale 也是
- **在家走家庭入口。** XDesk 在局域网里找到已配对的 Mac（Bonjour），经 Sill 的家庭入口连接：用家里的画质，丢帧后立刻恢复；在外面照旧连保存的地址。这个版本之前配对的 Mac 还没有识别密钥，要重新配对一次才能用上。
- **在外（Tailscale、公网）最多 60 fps**，并且按外屏当前的刷新率请求，不再按它支持的最高值。同样画质，数据量少一半。
- 解码好的画面马上上屏，不再等下一条消息；解码器按画面实际尺寸配置，并以全速运行。
- 断线约 6 秒就能发现（原来 12 秒）；换网络（Wi-Fi 换移动数据、VPN）立刻重连；画面正在显示时，重连间隔最多 2 秒。
- 重连到 Mac 正在推的画面时，不再让它重启采集（原来最多冻住 2 秒）；一次画质切换只发一条设置，不再是三条。
- 视频流量标优先级，提高显示和声音线程的优先级，显示 Mac 时让 Wi-Fi 进入低延迟模式；声音改用低延迟输出，卡顿之后延迟不再越积越长。

## 安全
- **无线投屏**：接收端要先证明自己持有配对（或你刚用配对码配好），XDesk 才接受。以前同一网络里的设备只要广播一个已知接收端的 id，就能拿到画面和声音、反向发送输入，还能顶替原来的配对。
- **X 服务器不再监听 TCP，也不再监听抽象 socket**（`-nolisten tcp -nolisten local`），手机上的其他应用都连不上了。Termux、proot、chroot 里的程序照旧经 Termux 自己的 socket 连接。
- **系统日志里不再出现剪贴板内容和输入的字符**（以前原生代码在调试级别写了进去），也不再记录完整的按键事件。
- **其他应用不能再卡死 XDesk**：以前递一个永不返回的 Binder 就能让它无响应。现在 X 连接的调用不在主线程上做、有超时，排队的数量有上限，Android 14 起只接受 Termux、XDesk、root 和 shell 发来的请求。X 服务器的 Binder 只回应 XDesk。
- **X 程序传来的显卡缓冲先检查尺寸和溢出**再映射，格式不对的直接拒绝，不会再让 X 服务器崩溃或写出界。
- **网页显示和 XDesk 接收器只在 Wi-Fi、有线和 USB 网络共享上开放**：不再接受本机、VPN（包括 Tailscale）和移动数据来的连接，和文档一直写的一样。没配对的设备不再能看到接收端的名字。
- 「发送到电脑」不管是哪个应用打开的，发送前都会先问你。原版 Termux:X11 的设置页不再对其他应用开放。

## 省电和速度
- **没东西连着时 X 服务器会睡下**：以前没有窗口也按手机刷新率每秒被叫醒 120 次，还每秒向 XDesk 发一次广播，一直不停。
- **不推流时不再持唤醒锁**：以前启动台开着、或者网页显示在监听，CPU 就一整夜不休眠。现在只有正在运行的桌面、应用会话或推流才持锁。
- 桌面的声音静音超过半秒就暂停，不再整场会话一直在播静音。
- 粘贴或输入长文本不再卡住手机（以前在主线程上每个字符等 2.5 ms）；每次按键不再固定等 60 ms；注入慢时，鼠标移动和滚动不再越积越多；实体鼠标和键盘的读取去掉了每 200 ms 一次的轮询；`dumpsys` 最多 2.5 秒跑一次，以前是每秒一次。
- 桌面窗口不再一直开着加速度计，看门狗从每秒 5 次降到每秒 1 次。手指按在静止画面上不再每秒整屏重画 120 次。X 服务器真正固定在原本打算的那几个 CPU 核上。通知面板只在你展开一条通知时才构建它的展开内容。

## 其他修复
- 从 Linux 复制的长文本到手机上会被截断，还可能让连接错位；几个线程同时写时事件会互相穿插。现在连接上的读写都是整条进行。
- 关掉再打开桌面窗口可能黑屏（旧连接断开时把新连接也清掉了）；连接过程中关掉的桌面窗口会一直重试并泄漏资源。
- 显示过 15% 低电量提示后，5% 的提示永远不会出现。
- 带着打开的回复框的横幅消失后，所有按键（包括 Esc）都被吞掉。
- 离开 XDesk 一分钟后再回来，手机屏可能停在 1% 亮度。
- 一次改几个设置时，处理逻辑会执行好几遍；修了几处窗口和线程的泄漏；加锁失败的缓冲会一直处于锁定状态，这个也修了。

## 升级
- 覆盖安装 0.6.3 的 APK，再在 Termux 里运行 `xdesk update`（X 服务器的启动参数变了）。XDesk 接收器也要更新。Mac 端除版本号外没有变化。
- 如果面板说通知进不来，在手机上给 XDesk 打开一次「自启动」。
- 想用 Mac 的家庭入口，要把这台 Mac 重新配对一次（设置 › Mac）。在那之前照旧连保存的地址。
- 如果你以前通过 VPN 或移动数据用网页显示或 XDesk 接收器，现在是故意不让连的，请改用 Wi-Fi、有线或 USB 网络共享。
