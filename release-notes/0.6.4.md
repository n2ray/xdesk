# XDesk 0.6.4

[中文说明见下方](#xdesk-064-1)

Notifications on the external screen, made dependable. It installs over 0.6.3.

## Fixed
- **The notification centre was sometimes empty.** Granting notification access is not enough: the system must also start XDesk's notification service. On HyperOS, after XDesk's process restarted (an update, the system closing it, a reboot), the system's attempt was refused because XDesk may not start itself, and no notification came in until something rebound it. XDesk now asks the system again when the service stops, when XDesk comes to the front, when the external screen appears and when the panel opens. While it is not connected, the panel says so and opens the phone's Autostart settings for you (Settings › Notifications shows it too).
- On every connection the centre is made the same as the phone's notifications: ones cleared meanwhile leave, new ones come in quietly.
- Clearing a notification from the external screen while the service was not connected made it vanish there but stay on the phone; it now stays in both.
- Notifications whose importance the system did not report were treated as silent and not shown. They are shown now.
- Some apps post only a group summary; those notifications were never shown. A summary now shows when its group has no messages.
- Chat notifications often showed only the app's name: the text now comes from the conversation's newest message, the big text or the inbox lines when the plain text is empty.
- Incoming calls (ongoing) now show.
- An empty panel says how many notifications on the phone are not shown here (silent, ongoing, or from apps set not to show).
- Apps set to "not shown" can be found again in the per-app settings.
- **App name only** now applies to the panel's list too, not just the banners, so a projector does not show what the banners hide. ▾ still shows a notification in full when you ask.
- The Do not disturb tile is on in any of the phone's DND modes, and turning it off and on returns to the mode you had, instead of switching to total silence.
- Opening a notification waits for the app to appear on the external screen (up to 2 seconds) instead of a fixed 0.6 seconds, so it is not started twice; when it cannot open there, or an action is refused, a banner says so.
- The panel keeps open and selected rows when the list changes, and no longer redraws for every battery percent. If its window could not be added, it can be opened again.

## Changed
- **Every "turn this permission on" looks the same now**, on the phone and on the external screen: an amber card with a key, "Turn on «X»", one line on why, one line on where it is in the phone's settings, and an amber button ("Turn on ›", or "Open on the phone ›" on the external screen). Red stays for errors, grey for notes. When you come back with the permission on, the card turns green and goes. This covers notification access, Autostart, Do Not Disturb access, notifications, display over other apps, running Termux commands, Shizuku, the camera for pairing a Mac and the setting USB tethering needs.
- Before the system asks for notifications, for running Termux commands or for the camera, XDesk now says what for first. The desktop window no longer puts a second, unexplained notification request on top, and phones before Android 13 are not asked at all.
- Allowed on the app's details page after refusing for good, starting a desktop, listing Termux or stopping goes on by itself when you come back. "Not now" on Do Not Disturb access is for this time only; the next tap asks again, and the external screen says to look at the phone.

## Upgrading
- Install the APK over 0.6.3. If the panel says notifications are not coming in, turn on Autostart for XDesk on the phone once. XDesk Receiver, the Mac app and the `xdesk` command are unchanged apart from the version number.

# XDesk 0.6.4

外屏通知改得可靠了。覆盖安装在 0.6.3 上即可。

## 修复
- **通知中心有时是空的。** 只给通知使用权还不够，系统还要启动 XDesk 的通知服务。HyperOS 上，XDesk 的进程一重启（装新版、被系统关掉、重启手机），系统去启动这个服务时会因为 XDesk 没有「自启动」权限而被拒绝，之后一条通知都进不来，直到碰巧重新连上。现在服务断开时、XDesk 回到前台时、外屏出现时、打开面板时，XDesk 都会再向系统请求。没连上时面板会明说，并能直接在手机上打开自启动设置（设置 › 通知里也有一行状态）。
- 每次连上时，通知中心都和手机上的通知对一遍：期间清掉的拿掉，新来的悄悄加进来。
- 服务没连上时在外屏上清除通知，外屏上没了、手机上却还在；现在两边都保留。
- 系统没报重要性的通知被当成静默通知不显示，现在会显示。
- 有的应用只发分组摘要，这类通知以前从不显示；现在组里没有消息时摘要会显示。
- 聊天通知常常只剩应用名：正文为空时，现在从会话的最新一条、长文本或收件箱样式的行里取。
- 来电（常驻通知）现在会显示。
- 面板为空时，会说明手机上还有几条没显示在这里（静默、常驻，或来自设为不显示的应用）。
- 设成「不显示」的应用，在按应用的设置里还能找到、改回来。
- **只显示应用名**现在也管面板的列表，不只是横幅，投影机上不会显示横幅藏起来的内容。点 ▾ 仍可看全文。
- 「勿扰」方块在手机的任何勿扰模式下都算开；关掉再打开会回到原来的模式，不再变成完全静音。
- 打开一条通知时，等应用真的出现在外屏上（最多 2 秒），不再固定等 0.6 秒，所以不会启动两次；开不到外屏或操作被应用拒绝时，会弹横幅说明。
- 列表变化时，展开和选中的条目保持不变；电量每变 1% 不再重画整个列表。面板窗口加不上时，下次还能再打开。

## 改动
- **所有「去开权限」的提示统一成一个样子**，手机屏和外屏都一样：琥珀色卡片加钥匙图标，写「需要打开「X」」，一行说为什么，一行说在手机设置的哪里，再加一个琥珀色按钮（「去开启 ›」，外屏上是「在手机上打开 ›」）。红色只用于出错，灰色只用于说明。开好权限回来，卡片变绿后消失。涵盖通知使用权、自启动、勿扰权限、通知、悬浮窗、运行 Termux 命令、Shizuku、配对 Mac 用的相机，以及 USB 网络共享要的系统设置权限。
- 系统弹窗问通知、运行 Termux 命令、相机权限之前，XDesk 先说明要它做什么。桌面窗口不再另外弹一个没有说明的通知权限框，Android 13 以前的手机不再问。
- 选过"不再询问"后在应用详情页里允许了，回来时原本要做的事（启动桌面、列出 Termux 环境、停止）会接着做。勿扰权限选"以后再说"只管这一次，下次点还会问，外屏上会提示去手机上看。

## 升级
- 覆盖安装 0.6.3 的 APK 即可。如果面板说通知进不来，在手机上给 XDesk 打开一次「自启动」。XDesk 接收器、Mac 端和 `xdesk` 命令除版本号外没有变化。
