# XDesk 0.6.0 beta 6

[中文说明见下方](#xdesk-060-测试版-6)

A **beta** for the receivers, the Mac app above all: one pointer instead of two, smooth trackpad scrolling, Retina screens, and the Dock and the Launchpad working with a computer's mouse. It installs over beta 5 and the betas before it, or over 0.5.1, which stays the latest stable release. Everything in beta 2 to beta 5 still applies.

**Update the Mac app too** (`XDesk-macos-arm64.dmg` below): the phone and the Mac app should be the same version. XDesk Receiver on a tablet or projector is worth updating as well (`xdesk-receiver.apk`). The `xdesk` command in Termux is the same as in beta 5.

## Fixed: two pointers on a computer
On the Mac app (and in a browser), the Mac's pointer and the one in the picture could both show. Three causes, all fixed:
- after X restarted (as it does after an app update), the X cursor was no longer hidden while the Mac's mouse placed the pointer;
- after using the phone's own touchpad, the Mac's pointer stayed beside the X cursor;
- with no Linux desktop, or over an Android app, the Launchpad's arrow showed beside the Mac's pointer.

macOS also showed its pointer again by itself when the window came to the front; the app now hides it again.

## Fixed: the Dock with a computer's mouse
Over the Linux desktop the Dock opened when the Mac's pointer rested on the bottom edge, but did not take clicks: they went to the desktop under it.

## New in the Mac app
- **Smooth trackpad scrolling**: fractions of a step with the trackpad's phases, instead of whole wheel steps. On the Linux desktop it scrolls smoothly, with momentum; to another Mac it is a trackpad gesture. XDesk Receiver's two-finger scrolling does the same.
- **Retina**: the app tells the phone its real density and refresh rate, so the Linux desktop and XDesk's own parts are sized as on the Mac; moving the window to a screen with another scale follows.
- **English**: the app follows the Mac's language, Chinese or English.
- **Version and updates**: Phone › About XDesk shows the version, and the app says when a newer one is out (Phone › Check for updates): a beta looks at betas, a stable version only at stable releases. It only opens the release page.
- **Busy**: while one phone streams, another gets "busy" instead of taking the Mac over.
- **Pairing limits**, as in XDesk Receiver: wrong codes lock an address out for a while, and too many pause pairing.
- **Two phones on USB**: each connects; before, neither did.
- **Japanese keyboards**: 英数 and かな reach the Mac again, so its input method can be switched in the window. F13 to F20 and the keypad's = reach the phone.
- The menu's Recents is now **Overview**, as in the bar.

## Upgrading
- Install the APK over beta 5, and the Mac app over the one you have.
- The protocol has one new message (SCROLL_FINE, PROTOCOL.md): older receivers and phones keep scrolling by whole steps.

# XDesk 0.6.0 测试版 6

接收端的测试版，主要是 Mac 版：两个光标变回一个，触控板平滑滚动，支持 Retina 屏，电脑鼠标可以用 Dock 和启动台了。可以直接覆盖安装在测试版 5 及之前的测试版上，也可以装在 0.5.1 上；0.5.1 仍是最新的正式版。测试版 2 到 5 的内容全部保留。

**Mac 版也要更新**（下面的 `XDesk-macos-arm64.dmg`）：手机和 Mac 两边的版本要一致。平板、投影机上的 XDesk Receiver 也建议更新（`xdesk-receiver.apk`）。Termux 里的 `xdesk` 命令和测试版 5 相同。

## 修复：电脑上两个光标
Mac 版（还有浏览器）上，Mac 自己的指针和画面里的光标可能同时出现。三个原因，都修好了：
- X 重启之后（更新 App 后就会重启），Mac 鼠标放置指针时，X 的光标不再被藏起；
- 用过手机自己的触控板后，Mac 的指针留在 X 光标旁边；
- 没有 Linux 桌面时，或者在安卓应用上，启动台的箭头旁边还有 Mac 的指针。

另外，窗口回到前台时 macOS 会自己把指针显示出来，现在会重新藏起。

## 修复：电脑鼠标用 Dock
在 Linux 桌面上，Mac 指针停在底边能呼出 Dock，但点不了：点击落到了下面的桌面上。

## Mac 版新功能
- **触控板平滑滚动**：发的是带触控板阶段的连续值，不再是一格一格的滚轮。Linux 桌面上滚动连续、有惯性；转给另一台 Mac 时就是触控板手势。XDesk Receiver 的双指滚动也一样。
- **Retina**：告诉手机真实的密度和刷新率，Linux 桌面和 XDesk 自己的界面按 Mac 上的大小显示；窗口拖到缩放倍数不同的屏上也会跟着变。
- **英文界面**：跟 Mac 的系统语言，中文或英文。
- **版本和更新**：菜单「手机 › 关于 XDesk」显示版本；有新版本时会提示（「手机 › 检查更新」），测试版只看测试版，正式版只看正式版。只打开发布页，不自动下载。
- **忙**：一部手机在投屏时，另一部手机连过来会收到「忙」，不再把 Mac 抢走。
- **配对限制**，和 XDesk Receiver 一样：配对码输错多次会暂时锁住那个地址，太多次会暂停配对。
- **USB 插两部手机**：每部都能连；以前两部都连不上。
- **日文键盘**：英数、かな又能交给 Mac，在窗口里也能切换输入法。F13 到 F20、小键盘的 = 能发到手机。
- 菜单里的「最近任务」改叫「**总览**」，和顶边按钮一致。

## 升级
- APK 覆盖安装在测试版 5 上，Mac 版覆盖安装旧版。
- 协议加了一条消息（SCROLL_FINE，见 PROTOCOL.md）：旧的接收端和手机照旧按整格滚动。
