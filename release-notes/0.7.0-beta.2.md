# XDesk 0.7.0 beta 2

[中文说明见下方](#xdesk-070-测试版-2)

Fixes found while testing beta 1. What's new in 0.7.0 is in the [beta 1 notes](https://github.com/n2ray/xdesk/releases/tag/v0.7.0-beta.1). It installs over beta 1 and over 0.6.6, which stays the latest stable release. Update XDesk Receiver and the Mac app too.

## Fixed
- The phone list's search field cut its hint (and what you typed) at half the box, losing the closing bracket. Narrow search fields now use a shorter hint.
- Settings › Permissions said autostart was off while it was on (MIUI and HyperOS).
- No external screen: the hint above the list and the Launchpad grid is the card from the design, not a line of orange text.
- Connect to a receiver: the search on the network stops after 15 seconds. If nothing turned up it says so, with **Search again**.
- Mac app, while waiting for the phone: the logo is back, and "allow USB debugging on the phone" and the Accessibility reminder show as cards under how to connect, instead of replacing it.

# XDesk 0.7.0 测试版 2

测试版 1 里发现的问题的修复。0.7.0 的新东西见[测试版 1 的说明](https://github.com/n2ray/xdesk/releases/tag/v0.7.0-beta.1)。可直接覆盖安装测试版 1 和 0.6.6，0.6.6 仍是最新稳定版。XDesk 接收器和 Mac 版也请一起更新。

## 修复
- 手机列表的搜索框，提示文字（和输入的字）到框的一半就被截断，末尾的括号不见了。窄的搜索框现在用短一点的提示。
- 设置 › 权限里，自启动明明开着却说没开（MIUI、HyperOS）。
- 没有外接屏时，列表和启动台上方的提示换成设计稿里的提示卡，不再是一行橙字。
- 连接接收器：在局域网里找 15 秒就停。没找到会写明，并给一个「再找一次」。
- Mac 版等待手机时：标志回来了；「在手机上点允许 USB 调试」和辅助功能的提醒以卡片显示在怎么连的下面，不再顶替它。
