# XDesk 0.6.0

[中文说明见下方](#xdesk-060)

The stable release of 0.6.0, a large rework of XDesk: one switchboard for the phone, the external screen and the Dock; Macs, Linux environments and receivers as devices; game passthrough; Termux alone is enough for a Linux desktop. It installs over 0.5.1 and over every 0.6.0 beta.

What came in the betas is described in their notes: [beta 1](https://github.com/n2ray/xdesk/releases/tag/v0.6.0-beta.1) (the switchboard, devices, Macs), [beta 2](https://github.com/n2ray/xdesk/releases/tag/v0.6.0-beta.2), [beta 3](https://github.com/n2ray/xdesk/releases/tag/v0.6.0-beta.3) and [beta 4](https://github.com/n2ray/xdesk/releases/tag/v0.6.0-beta.4) (game passthrough), [beta 5](https://github.com/n2ray/xdesk/releases/tag/v0.6.0-beta.5) (Termux alone, `xdesk doctor`), [beta 6](https://github.com/n2ray/xdesk/releases/tag/v0.6.0-beta.6) (the Mac app). Below is what is new since beta 6.

## New: the external screen fills, sharp, on any monitor
- Android 15 and later shrink some monitors' picture and add black bars (its "pixel anisotropy correction", for panels whose pixels are not quite square). XDesk now shows such a display on its whole panel, pixel for pixel, and keeps it so when the display changes. Monitors Android already shows correctly are left alone.
- **Desktop resolution** (the Linux desktop's): Auto (default), Native, 1080p, 720p. Auto draws at the panel's own size up to 1600 lines and scales a 4K panel down to a size that stays sharp; the lower ones save power and heat.
- **Screen kind** is guessed from the monitor (its size and whether it is a TV); XDesk asks only when it can not tell.

## New: the GPU composes the Linux desktop (glamor)
- On by default on Snapdragon (Adreno) GPUs, the ones it was tested on; other GPUs keep the old way for now (`TERMUX_X11_GLAMOR=1` turns it on there to try). 2D programs use about half the X server's CPU or less; OpenGL programs are faster than before (glmark2 651 against 381).
- If it can not start, X falls back to the old way by itself. `TERMUX_X11_GLAMOR=0` turns it off.
- The glamor port comes from [termux_on_dex](https://github.com/PietroCav501/termux_on_dex), GPL-3.0; thanks to its authors.

## New: the side panel instead of the bell
- The bell at the top right is gone, so it no longer covers apps' buttons there.
- Rest the pointer in the top-right corner: a side panel slides in, with switches at the top (the Launchpad, keys to the phone, where the sound plays, do not disturb, and game passthrough or a Mac's picture mode when they apply) and the notifications below. A new notification still shows as a banner.

## New: one clipboard for every device
- Copy on the phone, in an Android app on the external screen, on the Linux desktop, on a Mac or on a receiver, and paste anywhere else: XDesk keeps one clipboard for all of them (text, up to 256 KB).
- A copy in an Android app reaches the Mac and the Linux desktop at once. Android lets only the app in front read the clipboard; XDesk's activation (Shizuku or the built-in one) is told of each change instead, so nothing polls. Without activation it works while XDesk is in front, as before.
- **Share the clipboard** (under Connect to a receiver) turns it off for all devices.

## Improved: the touchpad
- **Two fingers scroll** and no longer zoom by mistake when they do not move quite straight; they zoom only when they move against each other. Scrolling holds its direction.
- **Three and four fingers** are more forgiving about landing together and about direction.
- The rules follow libinput's (thanks to its authors, MIT licence).

## Improved: the Dock
- **Easier to push**: any mostly-downward push at the bottom edge counts, not only a straight one; over the Linux desktop it opens sooner. A bar shows how far the push has gone.
- **Over Android apps**, where the pointer can not go past the edge, resting it at the bottom opens the Dock, with the bar growing as it rests.
- The Dock now shows over Android apps also while a Linux desktop runs behind them.

## Improved: other
- **The Launchpad keeps the pointer**: its arrow starts where the pointer was, and after it closes the pointer goes on from where the arrow was left.
- **Two-finger scroll speed** (Settings) applies to a mouse wheel on the phone; a computer's scrolling (the Mac app, a browser) goes as the computer made it.
- **A Mac with several displays** (Sill 0.6.2 or later): once connected, the launcher lists one desktop per display, and opening one shows that display. Not connected, it is one desktop as before.
- **XDesk Receiver** passes every finger to an Android app that takes touches, so games that need several fingers work on a touch-screen tablet.
- The external display runs at its fastest refresh rate while the pointer is in use.
- Fixes from a review of all input paths (X, Android apps, the Mac app, receivers).

## Upgrading
- Install the APK over 0.5.1 or any beta. If the desktop was running, XDesk asks once to restart the X server.
- Coming from 0.5.1: run the installer in Termux once; after that the app keeps the `xdesk` command up to date by itself.
  `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/n2ray/xdesk/xdesk/xdesk/install.sh | bash`
  From a beta, XDesk offers **Update command** once: tap it.
- Update the Mac app (`XDesk-macos-arm64.dmg`) and XDesk Receiver (`xdesk-receiver.apk`) too.
- The protocol has one new message (TOUCH, PROTOCOL.md); older receivers keep sending a single pointer.

# XDesk 0.6.0

0.6.0 正式版。这一版大改了 XDesk：手机、外接屏和 Dock 共用一个交换台；Mac、Linux 环境、接收端都是设备；游戏直通；只装 Termux 就能跑 Linux 桌面。可以直接装在 0.5.1 或任何一个 0.6.0 测试版上。

各测试版带来的内容见它们的说明：[测试版 1](https://github.com/n2ray/xdesk/releases/tag/v0.6.0-beta.1)（交换台、设备、Mac）、[测试版 2](https://github.com/n2ray/xdesk/releases/tag/v0.6.0-beta.2)、[测试版 3](https://github.com/n2ray/xdesk/releases/tag/v0.6.0-beta.3) 和 [测试版 4](https://github.com/n2ray/xdesk/releases/tag/v0.6.0-beta.4)（游戏直通）、[测试版 5](https://github.com/n2ray/xdesk/releases/tag/v0.6.0-beta.5)（只用 Termux、`xdesk doctor`）、[测试版 6](https://github.com/n2ray/xdesk/releases/tag/v0.6.0-beta.6)（Mac 版）。下面是测试版 6 之后的新内容。

## 新：任何显示器都满屏、清晰
- Android 15 起，系统会把一部分显示器的画面缩小、加上黑边（「像素各向异性校正」，针对像素不完全是正方形的面板）。XDesk 现在会让这种显示器整块面板都显示、像素一一对应，显示器状态变化后也保持住。系统本来就显示正确的显示器不去动它。
- **桌面分辨率**（Linux 桌面的）：自动（默认）、原生、1080p、720p。「自动」在 1600 行以内按面板原生尺寸绘制，4K 面板缩到仍然清晰的尺寸；低档更省电、更凉。
- **屏幕类型**按显示器自己的信息猜（尺寸、是不是电视），猜不准才问。

## 新：Linux 桌面由 GPU 合成（glamor）
- 骁龙（Adreno）显卡上默认打开，测试过的就是它；其他显卡暂时还用旧方式（想试可以设 `TERMUX_X11_GLAMOR=1` 打开）。2D 程序的 X 服务器 CPU 占用降到一半甚至更低；OpenGL 程序比以前快（glmark2 651 分，以前 381）。
- 起不来时 X 自动退回旧方式。设 `TERMUX_X11_GLAMOR=0` 可以关掉。

## 新：侧边栏代替铃铛
- 右上角的铃铛去掉了，不再挡住应用右上角的按钮。
- 指针停在右上角，侧边栏滑出：上面是开关（启动台、键盘交给手机、声音从哪里出、勿扰，用得上时还有游戏直通和 Mac 画质），下面是通知。新通知照样弹横幅提示。

## 新：所有设备一个剪贴板
- 在手机、外屏的安卓应用、Linux 桌面、Mac 或接收端上复制，到其他任何一处都能粘贴：XDesk 让它们共用一个剪贴板（文字，最长 256 KB）。
- 在安卓应用里复制的内容马上就能到 Mac 和 Linux 桌面。安卓只让前台应用读剪贴板，XDesk 改由激活服务（Shizuku 或内置的那个）在剪贴板变化时通知，不用反复去查。没激活时和以前一样，XDesk 在前台时才同步。
- 「连接接收器」里的「剪贴板同步」一个开关管所有设备。

## 改进：触摸板
- **双指滚动**手指没走直线也不会再误触发缩放；只有两指相向或相背移动才缩放。滚动会锁住方向。
- **三指、四指**对落指是否同时、方向是否准确更宽容。
- 规则参照 libinput（感谢它的作者，MIT 许可）。

## 改进：Dock
- **更容易推出来**：在底边大致向下推就算，不必垂直；Linux 桌面上出来得更快。有一条进度条显示推了多少。
- **在安卓应用上**指针推不出边缘，改成停在底边一会儿就打开，进度条随停留时间平滑增长。
- Linux 桌面在后台运行时，安卓应用上也会显示 Dock。

## 改进：其他
- **启动台不丢指针**：箭头从原来指针的位置出现，关掉后指针从箭头最后的位置接着动。
- **双指滚动速度**（设置）管手机上接的鼠标滚轮；电脑那边的滚动（Mac 版、浏览器）按电脑自己的设置。
- **多显示器的 Mac**（Sill 0.6.2 或更新）：连上之后，启动器里每台显示器各列一个桌面，点哪个就显示哪台。没连上时仍然只列一个桌面。
- **XDesk Receiver** 把每根手指都传给支持触摸的安卓应用，触屏平板上需要多指的游戏可以玩了。
- 指针在用时外接屏跑最高刷新率。
- 全面检查所有输入路径（X、安卓应用、Mac 版、接收端）后的修复。

## 升级
- APK 覆盖安装在 0.5.1 或任何测试版上。桌面正在运行的话，XDesk 会问一次是否重启 X 服务器。
- 从 0.5.1 升级：在 Termux 里运行一次安装命令，之后 App 会自己保持 `xdesk` 命令是最新的。
  `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/n2ray/xdesk/xdesk/xdesk/install.sh | bash`
  从测试版升级的，XDesk 会提示一次「更新命令」，点一下即可。
- Mac 版（`XDesk-macos-arm64.dmg`）和 XDesk Receiver（`xdesk-receiver.apk`）也一起更新。
- 协议加了一条消息（TOUCH，见 PROTOCOL.md）；旧的接收端照旧只发一个指针。
