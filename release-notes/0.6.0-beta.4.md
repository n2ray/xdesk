# XDesk 0.6.0 beta 4

[中文说明见下方](#xdesk-060-测试版-4)

A fix for game passthrough from [beta 3](https://github.com/n2ray/xdesk/releases/tag/v0.6.0-beta.3). It installs over beta 3, beta 2, beta 1 or 0.5.1, which stays the latest stable release. Everything in beta 2 and beta 3 still applies.

## Fixed
- **Game passthrough no longer turns itself on.** In beta 3, GeForce NOW, Steam Link and Moonlight entered it automatically. That fought the touch on the phone that ends it: when the app changed pages (GeForce NOW going from its store to a game) it counted as a new app, so passthrough came straight back, and it took two presses of Ctrl+Alt+G to really get in. Now only **Ctrl+Alt+G** turns it on, and it goes in and out with a single press.

## Game passthrough, as it is now
- **Ctrl+Alt+G** with an Android app on the external screen: the app gets the real keyboard and mouse (cloud gaming such as GeForce NOW, Steam Link, Moonlight). XDesk's cursor hides.
- **Ctrl+Alt+G** again, or **a touch on the phone**, ends it.
- The keyboard and mouse must be connected to the phone. The picture can still go to a wireless receiver.

## Upgrading
- Install the APK over beta 3. The `xdesk` command, the receivers and the Mac app are the same as in beta 2 and beta 3.

# XDesk 0.6.0 测试版 4

修复[测试版 3](https://github.com/n2ray/xdesk/releases/tag/v0.6.0-beta.3) 的游戏直通。可以直接覆盖安装在测试版 3、测试版 2、测试版 1 或 0.5.1 上；0.5.1 仍是最新的正式版。测试版 2 和测试版 3 的内容全部保留。

## 修复
- **游戏直通不再自己打开。** 测试版 3 里 GeForce NOW、Steam Link、Moonlight 会自动进入直通，结果和「碰手机退出」打架：应用换页面时（GeForce NOW 从商店页进到游戏）被当成新应用，直通马上又开了，要按两次 Ctrl+Alt+G 才真正进去。现在只有 **Ctrl+Alt+G** 能打开，按一次进、按一次出。

## 现在的游戏直通
- 外屏上是安卓应用时按 **Ctrl+Alt+G**：应用直接拿到真实的键鼠（GeForce NOW、Steam Link、Moonlight 这类云游戏），XDesk 的光标藏起来。
- 再按一次 **Ctrl+Alt+G**，或者**碰一下手机**，退出。
- 键鼠要连在手机上；画面照样可以无线投到接收端。

## 升级
- APK 覆盖安装在测试版 3 上。`xdesk` 命令、接收端和 Mac App 和测试版 2、3 相同。
