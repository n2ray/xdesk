# XDesk 0.6.3

[中文说明见下方](#xdesk-063-1)

The phone as a gamepad. It installs over 0.6.2.

## New
- **Gamepad:** while an Android app is on the display, tap **Gamepad** at the top of the phone screen and the phone turns sideways into a gamepad: two sticks, d-pad, A B X Y, bumpers, triggers, Back, Start, Home, L3 and R3. Games see a real wired Xbox 360 controller (made in the kernel, as a plugged-in one would be), so any app that takes a gamepad can use it, GeForce NOW included. Tried on the test phone: Genshin Impact in GeForce NOW on the external display plays normally. Several fingers at once; sticks centre where you touch them (or stay fixed); presses vibrate (can be turned off).
- **Your own layouts:** from the gamepad's ⋯ menu, drag controls, make them larger or smaller, hide them, make the whole pad fainter or bolder. Keep several layouts (Standard and Simple come with it); each app gets the one it used last. Copy a layout to the clipboard and paste it on another phone.
- Back (or Leave the gamepad in its menu) returns to the touchpad. The gamepad stays connected for the session, so the game does not pause when you leave it. Details in [GAMEPAD.md](https://github.com/n2ray/xdesk/blob/xdesk/GAMEPAD.md).

## Fixed
- **Mac app:** messages over the picture, such as the one for the low-latency switch, were white text with nothing behind them and could not be read on a light picture. They now have a dark backing.

## Upgrading
- Install the APK over 0.6.2. XDesk Receiver and the `xdesk` command are unchanged apart from the version number. Update the Mac app for the fixed messages.

# XDesk 0.6.3

手机当手柄。覆盖安装在 0.6.2 上即可。

## 新功能
- **手柄：** 外接屏上是安卓应用时，点手机界面顶上的「手柄」，手机横过来变成一个手柄：两个摇杆、方向键、A B X Y、肩键、扳机、Back、Start、Home、L3 和 R3。游戏看到的是一个真的 Xbox 360 有线手柄（在内核里建的，和插上一个一样），所以凡是支持手柄的应用都能用，包括 GeForce NOW。在测试机上试过：外接屏上 GeForce NOW 里玩原神，操作正常。可以多指同时按；摇杆以按下的位置为中心（也可以固定）；按下时振动（可以关）。
- **自己的布局：** 在手柄的「⋯」菜单里拖动控件、放大缩小、隐藏，调整整体浓淡。可以存几套布局（自带「标准」和「简洁」），每个应用自动用它上次用的那套。布局可以复制到剪贴板，粘贴到另一台手机。
- 返回键（或菜单里的「退出手柄」）回到触摸板。手柄在整个会话里保持连接，退出手柄时游戏不会暂停。详见 [GAMEPAD.md](https://github.com/n2ray/xdesk/blob/xdesk/GAMEPAD.md)。

## 修复
- **Mac 端：** 画面上的提示（比如低延迟模式的开关提示）是白字、背后什么都没有，画面亮时看不见。现在有深色底。

## 升级
- 覆盖安装 0.6.2 的 APK 即可。XDesk 接收器和 `xdesk` 命令除版本号外没有变化。Mac 端更新后提示才有深色底。
