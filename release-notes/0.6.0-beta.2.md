# XDesk 0.6.0 beta 2

[中文说明见下方](#xdesk-060-测试版-2)

The second **beta** of 0.6.0. It brings the phone's notifications to the external screen, the menus of Mac apps, and a single cursor style, and fixes what beta 1 testing found. It installs over 0.6.0 beta 1 or 0.5.1, which stays the latest stable release.

## New features

### Notifications on the external screen
- The phone's notifications appear on the external screen as **banners** at the top right, and stay in a **notification centre**.
- Open the centre in three ways: push the pointer out through the **top-right corner**, click the **bell**, or press **Ctrl+Alt+N**. The bell shows how many are unread.
- The centre shows the **time, date and the phone's battery** on one line, your notifications grouped by app, **Do not disturb** and **Clear all**. Click a notification to open its app on the external screen; reply where the app allows it. The keyboard works it too: ↑↓, Enter, Delete, R (reply), Esc.
- **Low battery banners** at 15% and 5% while not charging.
- Settings › **Notifications** (also on the first settings page): what banners say (everything, app name only, none), per app (banner + centre, centre only, hidden), the bell, the low-battery banners.
- It needs Android's **notification access** for XDesk. XDesk offers it once when an external screen is first used. If the system's switch is grey or says "Restricted setting" (apps not installed from a store, Android 13 and later), the Notifications page explains the way through and opens XDesk's app info page. Without the access everything works as before.
- Nothing of a notification is stored or sent anywhere; it lives in memory only. Not shown on a car screen.

### Menus of Mac apps
- With a single Mac window or app on the external screen, a **≡ button** sits at the top left, the twin of the bell. Click it, push the pointer out through the **top-left corner**, or press **Ctrl+Alt+Space** to open the app's **menu bar**.
- Menus drop down as on the Mac: titles, shortcuts, check marks, greyed items, separators and submenus. The item under the pointer is highlighted; the keyboard works it with ←→ ↑↓ Enter Esc.
- On the phone, a Mac item's card has a **Menu** row with the same menus as a list.
- It needs Sill to be allowed under **Accessibility** on the Mac; the bar says so when it is not.

### Closing and quitting
- **Quitting a Mac app really quits it** (from its menu, ⌘Q, or **Quit** on the app's item under Running), so apps no longer pile up on the Mac.
- A Mac window's card has **Close** (the window only); a Mac app's has **Quit**.
- One rule when what is on the screen goes away (closed, quit, the X desktop restarted): the screen goes back to what was there before, or else to the Launchpad.

### One cursor
- The Launchpad's pointer is the same arrow as everywhere else, no longer a blue dot.
- The X side gets a black arrow with a white edge, sized for the screen like XDesk's own (`pkg install adwaita-icon-theme` in Termux). It applies only to sessions XDesk starts; a desktop you start some other way keeps its cursor.
- Receivers know whether the picture already has a cursor, so a receiver no longer shows two; a tablet's mouse over an Android app gets the phone's arrow.

### Car (Android Auto)
- Everything is sized for the distance of a car screen.
- Sound follows Android Auto being connected.
- No Launchpad on the car; the screen size setting says it does not apply there.

### Experiments
- **Split screen prototype** (Settings › Experiments): two Android apps side by side on the external screen, each on a display of its own, the mouse working the side it is over. A test to judge smoothness and input, not a finished feature.

### `xdesk` command
- `xdesk add NAME 'COMMAND' --root DIR --launch 'PREFIX'` for chroot environments: where their apps are listed from and how they are started.

## Improvements and fixes
- **The Dock opens again over Android apps** by pushing the pointer against the bottom edge (broken since the notification work after beta 1).
- **Fixed a crash** when what is on the screen goes away, for example when the X desktop is restarted.
- The Launchpad opened over an Android app turns pages with the mouse wheel again.
- The pointer stays above the notification centre, the Mac menus and the Dock instead of going under them, over every content.
- The corner buttons (≡ and the bell) have a dark plate, so they read over a light picture; both are as high as the Mac menu bar.

## Known limits
- The receiver paths (XDesk Receiver, the web page, the Mac app) were not retested on real devices for this beta.
- The split screen is a prototype: input focus between the two sides and the frame rate shown are rough.
- Mac sound is not synchronised with the picture.
- Windows PCs, the file shelf and receiver protocol v3 come later.

## Upgrading
- Install the APK over beta 1 or 0.5.1. If the desktop was running during the update, XDesk asks once to restart the X server (programs on it close).
- Run the installer again in Termux to get the new `xdesk` command:
  `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/n2ray/xdesk/xdesk/xdesk/install.sh | bash`
- For the X cursor: `pkg install adwaita-icon-theme` in Termux.
- On receivers, install the new `xdesk-receiver.apk`, and on a Mac the new `XDesk-macos-arm64.dmg`.
- Mac support is a client for [Sill](https://github.com/Saffsanity/sill) by Noah Saffer, following [Sall](https://github.com/n2ray/sall), a third-party Android client for Sill by n2ray. Neither XDesk nor Sall is an official Sill app.

# XDesk 0.6.0 测试版 2

0.6.0 的第二个**测试版**：把手机通知带到外屏，加上 Mac 应用的菜单和统一的光标，并修好测试版 1 测出来的问题。可以直接覆盖安装在 0.6.0 测试版 1 或 0.5.1 上；0.5.1 仍是最新的正式版。

## 新功能

### 外屏上的通知
- 手机的通知会在外屏右上角弹出**横幅**，并留在**通知中心**里。
- 三种方式打开通知中心：把鼠标推出**右上角**、点**铃铛**、按 **Ctrl+Alt+N**。铃铛上显示未读数。
- 通知中心顶上一行是**时间、日期和手机电量**，下面是按应用分组的通知，还有**勿扰**和**全部清除**。点一条通知会在外屏打开对应的应用；应用允许时可以直接回复。也能用键盘：↑↓、Enter、Delete、R（回复）、Esc。
- 未充电时电量到 15% 和 5% 会弹**低电量横幅**。
- 设置 ›「**通知**」（设置首页也有）：横幅显示什么（全部、只显示应用名、不弹）、按应用（横幅 + 通知中心、只进通知中心、不显示）、铃铛、低电量横幅。
- 需要给 XDesk 开 Android 的「**通知使用权**」。第一次接外屏时 XDesk 会问一次。系统开关是灰的、或提示「受限设置」时（Android 13 起，不是从应用商店装的应用都会这样），「通知」页里写了怎么放行，并能直接打开 XDesk 的应用详情页。不开这个权限，其他功能照旧。
- 通知内容不落盘、不外传，只在内存里。车机屏上不显示。

### Mac 应用的菜单
- 外屏上是单个 Mac 窗口或应用时，左上角有一个 **≡ 按钮**，和铃铛是一对。点它、把鼠标推出**左上角**、或按 **Ctrl+Alt+Space**，打开这个应用的**菜单栏**。
- 下拉菜单和 Mac 上一样：标题、快捷键、勾选、灰色的不可用项、分隔线、子菜单。鼠标经过的项会高亮；也能用键盘 ←→ ↑↓ Enter Esc 操作。
- 手机上 Mac 项目的卡片里有「**菜单**」一行，以列表显示同样的菜单。
- 需要在 Mac 的「**辅助功能**」里允许 Sill；没允许时菜单栏会提示。

### 关闭和退出
- **退出 Mac 应用会真的退出**（菜单、⌘Q、或「正在运行」里应用项的「退出」），Mac 上不会再越开越多。
- Mac 窗口的卡片是「**关闭**」（只关这个窗口）；Mac 应用的卡片是「**退出**」。
- 外屏上的内容没了（关掉、退出、重启 X 桌面）时，统一回到之前的内容，没有就回启动台。

### 统一的光标
- 启动台的鼠标和其他地方一样是箭头，不再是蓝色小圆点。
- X 这边用带白边的黑箭头，大小和 XDesk 自己的界面一致（Termux 里 `pkg install adwaita-icon-theme`）。只对 XDesk 启动的会话生效；用别的方式起的桌面保留自己的光标。
- 接收端知道画面里是否已经带光标，不会再出现两个光标；平板的鼠标在安卓应用上显示手机的箭头。

### 车机（Android Auto）
- 所有界面按车机屏的观看距离放大。
- 声音跟随 Android Auto 的连接切换。
- 车机上没有启动台；屏幕尺寸设置会注明在车机上不适用。

### 实验功能
- **分屏原型**（设置 › 实验）：外屏左右并排两个安卓应用，各自在独立的显示器上，鼠标在哪边就操作哪边。用来判断流畅度和输入方式，还不是正式功能。

### `xdesk` 命令
- `xdesk add 名字 '命令' --root 目录 --launch '前缀'`，给 chroot 环境指定从哪里列出应用、怎么启动它们。

## 改进和修复
- **安卓应用上又能推底边呼出 Dock 了**（测试版 1 之后做通知功能时弄坏的）。
- **修复崩溃**：外屏上的内容没了（比如重启 X 桌面）时 XDesk 会崩溃。
- 在安卓应用上打开的启动台又能用鼠标滚轮翻页了。
- 在所有内容上，鼠标都会留在通知中心、Mac 菜单和 Dock 的上面，不再被盖住。
- 角落的两个按钮（≡ 和铃铛）加了深色底板，在浅色画面上也看得清，高度和 Mac 菜单栏一致。

## 已知局限
- 这次没有在真机上重测接收端（XDesk 接收器、网页端、Mac App）。
- 分屏只是原型：两边之间的输入焦点、显示的帧率都还粗糙。
- Mac 的声音和画面没有同步。
- Windows 电脑、文件中转架、接收端协议 v3 以后再做。

## 升级
- APK 直接覆盖安装在测试版 1 或 0.5.1 上。更新时桌面在运行的话，XDesk 会问一次是否重启 X（上面的程序会关闭）。
- 在 Termux 里重新运行安装脚本，拿到新的 `xdesk` 命令：
  `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/n2ray/xdesk/xdesk/xdesk/install.sh | bash`
- X 光标：Termux 里 `pkg install adwaita-icon-theme`。
- 接收端装新的 `xdesk-receiver.apk`，Mac 上装新的 `XDesk-macos-arm64.dmg`。
- Mac 支持是 Noah Saffer 的 [Sill](https://github.com/Saffsanity/sill) 的客户端，参照了 n2ray 写的第三方 Sill 安卓客户端 [Sall](https://github.com/n2ray/sall)。XDesk 和 Sall 都不是 Sill 官方应用。
